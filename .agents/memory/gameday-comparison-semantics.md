description: Approved meaning of operational and commercial comparisons, and fair comparison boundaries.
---

GameDay operational revenue comprises F&B, Merchandising, Hospitality, Parking, and Experience. Commercial GameDay revenue adds Sponsorship, and adds Ticketing only when the existing Include Ticketing choice is enabled. Keep TV outside this commercial definition.

**Why:** The user approved a Ticketing-style GameDay comparison aimed at explaining whether performance changes come from audience size or operational revenue per person. Sponsorship allocations must not be presented as spending by spectators.

**How to apply:** Keep operational €/person separate from commercial €/person. Normalize volume comparisons by game when selections have unequal sizes, and calculate €/person from total revenue divided by total audience. Preserve same-tier historical comparisons and align YTD by chronological home fixtures without shifting past missing figures. Do not infer profit or margin without comparable match-level costs.
