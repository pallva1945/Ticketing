---
name: Tier-over-years meaning
description: User's intended semantics for ticketing tier comparisons.
---

“Tier over the years” means selecting one tier (for example, Tier 1) and comparing that same tier against its results in previous seasons. Volume measures should be normalized per matching game so seasons with different numbers of games can be compared; yield and percentage metrics retain their ratio semantics.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected an initial interpretation that compared two different tiers side by side.

**How to apply:** Keep this same-tier historical comparison when extending ticketing filters, charts, or print reports; do not turn it back into a Tier A versus Tier B selector.
