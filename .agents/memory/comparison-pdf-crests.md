---
name: Comparison PDF crests
description: Visual accuracy and print reliability constraints for team and competition badges.
---

Use only verified, locally served crests in comparison PDFs. Show a rival crest only when it matches a specific opponent represented by the report; a multi-opponent summary must not be branded as one rival. Match competition badges to the actual competition, and retain readable text if a verified image is unavailable.

**Why:** Public image searches returned football clubs, unrelated teams, historical variants, and even generic placeholders for some basketball rivals; externally hosted images also may fail or print before loading. A plausible but wrong crest is worse than an explicit text label.

**How to apply:** Verify image content visually before adding a new mapping, keep the PDF self-contained through local assets where possible, and wait for images to load before invoking print.
