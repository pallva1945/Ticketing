---
name: CRM full-season capacity
description: Business boundary between full-season subscriptions and partial-season products in Fixed/Flexible.
---

Only complete season subscriptions belong in Fixed. Renewal/pre-emption ("Prelazione") represents a complete subscription, not a pack. A product being called "Abbonamento" or sold through ABB/CORP does not establish that it is Fixed.

**Why:** The source changed subscription naming between seasons, including trailing punctuation. Simply matching the word "abbonamento" would also reclassify historical "7eventi" and "Girone di Ritorno" products, contrary to the requirement to preserve historical figures.

**How to apply:** Inspect distinct real event names before extending recognized subscription families. Retain partial subscriptions and packs as Flexible unless a separate business-rule change is approved. Compare historical calculations before and after classification changes, and use one rule for local filters and server aggregates.
