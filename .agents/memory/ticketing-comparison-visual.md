name: Ticketing comparison visual preference
description: Confirmed visual direction for the ticketing comparison charts.
---

For ticketing comparisons, prefer vertical columns with an actual calculated trend line rather than horizontal bars or a line connecting observed values. Keep metric selection flexible, including detailed zone and giveaway measures.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected horizontal graphs and point-to-point joins, then affirmed the revised direction as “Espectacular” before requesting another comparison mode.

**How to apply:** When revisiting ticketing comparisons, preserve this distinction between a regression-based trend and a line joining observed values; do not infer a temporal trend for arbitrary A/B scenarios.

For the two growth charts in the six-chart comparison view and PDF, prefer Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) over year-over-year growth, with the full English term visible.

**Why:** The user explicitly changed the requested defaults from YoY to CAGR and specified the English expansion to match the app.

**How to apply:** Keep Revenue and Yield CAGR distinct, preserve their calculated trend lines for seasonal series, and avoid presenting arbitrary Custom A/B as annual growth.
