For the 26/27 season, the user supplied zone-level GameDay capacities as **available seats**, not the number of fixed seats. Derive fixed seats as full zone capacity minus the supplied GameDay figure. Do not further deduct per-game corporate allocations from those supplied available-seat figures. Preserve earlier seasons' capacity rules.

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified “Esta es la GameDay capacity” when supplying the figures after reviewing the previous approach, which had reused 25/26 fixed seats and sometimes deducted additional corporate seats.

**How to apply:** When modifying 26/27 occupancy, comparisons, forecasts, or availability, treat those inputs as the intended GameDay denominators and avoid double deductions. Ask for fresh user-supplied numbers before changing that business meaning.
