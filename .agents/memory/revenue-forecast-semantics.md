name: Revenue forecast semantics and guaranteed fixtures
description: Keep headline forecasts comparable with season targets; the 2026/27 revenue fixture baseline includes BCL and a paid friendly.
---

Revenue Verticals and Season Pacing must compare the full-season forecast with the full-season target in their headline percentage, currency variance, and status colour. Any year-to-date versus expected-to-date measure must be explicitly labelled YTD and presented separately.

**Why:** The user rejected a display that called performance behind while the adjacent full-season forecast exceeded its target. They requested one consistent interpretation rather than making readers reconcile different periods.

**How to apply:** Do not place YTD shortfall amounts under forecast-versus-target percentages or colour forecast headlines according to YTD pace. Keep strategic forecast summaries consistent with the same comparison. Preserve real forecasts from known annual/monthly sources even before the first played game; unavailable forecasts remain unavailable.

The 2026/27 Ticketing and GameDay revenue baseline is 19 home fixtures: 15 LBA, 3 guaranteed BCL, and 1 friendly that earned revenue. More European games are possible but not yet guaranteed.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed the three guaranteed BCL fixtures and the revenue-generating friendly, and said both Ticketing and GameDay must count them.

**How to apply:** Use this baseline consistently in revenue pacing and forecasts without increasing revenue budgets or inventing additional games. Keep historical season assumptions intact. Upcoming sales must still be excluded from played-game averages. If more than the baseline have actually been played, forecasts must not project less than already earned revenue.
